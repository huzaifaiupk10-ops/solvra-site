What exactly does SOLVRA build?
SOLVRA builds three categories of digital systems: custom AI agents and automation workflows that run business processes; premium websites and web applications designed to convert; and AI chatbots trained on your specific business data. Most projects combine at least two of these.
Can you build an AI chatbot for my business?
Yes. Every chatbot is trained on your products, services, tone of voice, and FAQ data using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). It can capture leads, answer questions, book calls, and route conversations based on intent, without you managing it.
Can you automate my business workflows?
Yes. After a discovery call to map your current workflows, we identify which steps are manual, repetitive, or error-prone, then build automation using n8n, Make, or custom code that connects your tools and handles those steps automatically.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. Redesigns are one of the most common projects. The process starts with an audit of your current site, identifying what to keep, what to improve, and where the conversion and design gaps are, before we rebuild anything.
How long does a project take?
A starter website takes 2 to 3 weeks. A business system with AI agents and automation typically runs 4 to 8 weeks. Full builds are scoped individually. Timelines are always agreed upfront and tracked transparently throughout.
