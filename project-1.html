Overview

SYLO is a conceptual footwear brand identity created for a modern performance-shoe company. The project combines bold art direction, product visualization, and campaign design to establish a sleek, energetic, and recognizable brand presence.

The visual system uses a high-contrast red, white, and black palette, geometric typography, technical design elements, and dynamic product compositions. Multiple campaign posters were developed to showcase the footwear from different angles while maintaining a consistent identity across every touchpoint.
